# ART DIRECTION — THE VIVARIUM (WEB EDITION)

## 1. Visual Pillars
1. **Cinematic Nature Macro-Photography × Premium Stylized Illustration**:
   Inspired by natural science documentaries (*Microcosmos*), high-end formicarium acrylic setups, and cozy biological games (*Apico*, *Oxygen Not Included*, *Tiny Glade*).
2. **Coherent Color Palette**:
   - **Outworld / Canopy**: Soft golden sunlight, verdant forest foliage (`#2E6F40`), azure water reflections (`#2A75A0`, `#4AB1D8`), wildflower blossoms (clover whites, daisy yellows, bellflower lavenders).
   - **Wild Honeycomb**: Rich translucent amber (`#E5A125`, `#FFC043`), golden honey cells with soft rim lighting.
   - **Subterranean Formicarium**: Warm chocolate loam (`#281810`), damp peat strata (`#190E08`), and soft **red observation illumination** (`#6B1C10`) simulating professional red-film acrylic observation nests.

## 2. Eliminating Past Rendering Flaws
| Previous Godot Flaw | Web PixiJS Solution |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Grid Checkerboard Seams** | **Continuous Soil Canvas + Dynamic Masking**: World-space continuous soil layers with smooth gradient/distance-field erosion at tunnel edges. Zero tile cuts. |
| **Static Chamber Collage PNGs** | **Procedurally Formed Caverns**: Cavities are dynamically hollowed out from occupancy data, revealing warm glowing earthen backdrops without hard rectangular stamps. |
| **Microscopic Unreadable Ants** | **Calibrated Insect Sprites**: Clear, high-contrast chitin bodies (head, thorax, gaster segments, jointed legs, twitching antennae) with crisp vector/spritesheet rendering and smooth procedural gait. |
| **Lifeless Environment** | **Active Fauna & Dynamics**: Shimmering animated lake with swimming fish and ripples; gentle wind in canopy leaves; bees dynamically dipping into flower nectaries. |
